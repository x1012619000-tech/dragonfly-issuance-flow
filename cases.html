    MPChat x Dragonfly

    Case 01 / Cooperation
    Aviation × Web3 payments

    MPChat x
    Dragonfly

    A bot entry, an embedded flight-booking mini app, stablecoin payment and order notifications come together as one travel-service path inside MPChat.

    01 · App entry

    Inside the chat users already use

    MPChat hosts the chat, the bot and the mini-app entry, so travel services fit into an existing app journey. Search “Airline” to find the flight bot and start the service.

    02 · Flight service

    Search, compare, pay in USDT

    Dragonfly powers the flight-booking scene: route, dates, passengers and cabin in one place; fares shown tax-included in USDT with local times, duration and baggage notes.

    03 · Messages & orders

    Progress returns to the chat

    The bot reports payment and ticketing status, with shortcuts to the order, order details and the e-ticket number, plus pre-departure reminders.

    User journey
    Search the bot→Start the assistant→Search flights→Choose a flight→Payment→Bot notification→Order details
